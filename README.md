# uac-forecasting-dashboard
The Unaccompanied Alien Children (UAC) Program managed by the Department of Health and
Human Services (HHS) faces complex operational challenges due to volatile, non-linear intake
volumes from Customs and Border Protection (CBP) custody. Historically, reactive capacity
planning has yielded recurring system stresses, including acute overcrowding risks, systemic staff
burnout, and prolonged lengths of stay for minors. This paper establishes a rigorous framework
leveraging high-quality daily time-series telemetry to shift operations from a reactive to a proactive
configuration. We systematically review methods for time-series structural decomposition, feature
engineering methodologies involving flow-based pressure signals, and predictive multi-horizon
forecasting benchmarks comparing classical statistical models (ARIMA/SARIMA) against robust
machine learning regressors. Crucially, we outline an implementable blueprint for interactive
decision-support applications (Streamlit) optimized for real-time healthcare infrastructure planners.
